## Executive Summary

Cycle 9 shipped monetization nudges (family plan, win-back banner, trust microcopy). Cycle 10 adds the next logical monetization lever: a **cancellation survey + save offer flow** for users who already subscribed but want to cancel. Since the app does not currently drive cancellation through StoreKit/Google Play UI, we build an in-app "Manage subscription" entry point that collects intent and presents matched retention offers as soft nudges before redirecting to the platform subscription settings.

**New critical findings:**
1. **No cancellation feedback loop** — users who cancel leave silently; the team learns nothing about churn reasons.
2. **No save offer surface** — common patterns (discount, pause, free month) are not available in-app.
3. **"Bought already?" / restore path only** — there is no "Manage subscription" CTA in the paywall or profile.
4. **Retention offer compliance** — California ARL (July 2025) and FTC/ROSCA require the "cancel" option to remain visible alongside any offer.

## Competitor UX Benchmark (cycle 10)

| Source | Pattern to borrow |
|---|---|
| [Retainly cancel flow best practices](https://getretainly.app/blog/cancel-flow-best-practices) | Survey-first, matched offer, 25–45% save rate target |
| [Churnkey first-screen save offer](https://churnkey.co/growth/library/first-screen-save-offer) | Offer-first variant with explicit accept/decline |
| [RevenueCat Customer Center RN](https://www.revenuecat.com/docs/tools/customer-center/customer-center-react-native) | Native customer center with feedback survey + promotional offers |
| [ZeroSettle cancel flow](https://docs.zerosettle.io/iap/cancel-flow) | Backend-driven questionnaire → offer → outcome |
| [BluStream cancel survey](https://blustream.ai/blog/subscription-cancel-survey-build-exit-surveys-that-work) | 5 canonical reasons, required free-text for "missing feature" |

**Key patterns to adopt this cycle:**
- Add a **"Manage subscription"** button on `SubscriptionScreen` and `ProfileScreen`.
- Launch an in-app **cancellation survey modal** with 5 canonical reasons.
- Show a **matched save offer** per reason (discount, pause, free month, support) with the real "Still cancel" button always visible.
- Persist survey responses locally in AsyncStorage for later analysis.
- Localize all copy in `en` and `ru`.
